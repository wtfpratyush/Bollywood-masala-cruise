OUR STORY

The Journey of Bollywood Masala Cruise

The Bollywood Masala Cruise was born from a passion for Indian cultural celebrations and a love for the open sea. Our founders envisioned an extraordinary luxury holiday that blends the joyous energy of Bollywood music, authentic regional cuisines, and top-tier hospitality on world-class liners.

Every voyage is curated to feel like a floating festival — bringing together families, friends, and Bollywood enthusiasts across the globe for an unforgettable experience.
